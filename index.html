Pitches use just-intonation ratios with Sa set to C (261.6 Hz), over a Sa–Pa drone. In performance every raga also carries gamakas (oscillations and slides between notes) that no scale drawing can show, so treat each wheel as the raga's skeleton. Mood associations are traditional and vary between schools.
